# Brag Plan: TD2 Loot Advisor

## What is this app?
TD2 Loot Advisor is a local, screen-reading companion for The Division 2: it reads an item tooltip, compares it with the active build and returns a keep/consider/review decision without touching game memory.

## The angle
Turn a cluttered loot card into a calm SHD terminal decision in seconds. The video should feel like a field agent handing an item to an evidence desk: scan, compare, then act with confidence.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark tactical terminal boots with the real product name and a large line: “HOVER → F8 → KNOW.” A cyan scan line crosses a compact item-card silhouette while the orange/cyan HUD accent snaps on.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A realistic in-app item card arrives in the Panel and its OCR readout resolves into item name, slot and roll-quality bars.
- The active-build comparison appears beside it with concise requirement checks and a visible REVIEW-safe state for uncertain OCR.
- A quick route through Builds / Stash / Tasks shows the actual product breadth: build planning, stash audit and current task tracking.

## Outro / punchline
The terminal settles on “KEEP YOUR BUILD. REVIEW THE UNKNOWN.” followed by “TD2 LOOT ADVISOR · v9.1.5.35” and the bundled TD2 icon.

## User flow worth showing
Hover an item in-game → press F8 / run the local OCR scan → inspect the build-aware verdict and missing-piece guidance in Panel/Stash.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: restrained SHD field-terminal boot sequence with crisp evidence beats
- Interpretation: slow enough for readable UI, with two strong scan/reveal accents and minimal glitch; no generic SaaS language.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0a0d12
- Accent: #36e0c4 (with restrained Division-like orange/cyan scan accents)
- Text: #eef2f6
- Display font: Segoe UI Semibold
- Body font: Segoe UI
- Strongest visual element: dark graphite cards, teal selection state, cyan HUD brackets and OCR/build status panels.

## Share copy (draft)
TD2 Loot Advisor turns one hovered Division 2 item into a build-aware decision: scan locally, compare against your build, and keep the unknown in REVIEW instead of guessing.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3, low and steady
- Music treatment: 0.22–0.28 volume, short fade-in, duck slightly under the final lockup, fade out cleanly.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset JSON copied into composition assets; target strong cues near 2.0s, 8.0s and 17.5s. Sequential UI rows should use every other beat for readability.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the scan halo and card presence breathe with RMS/bass, never a waveform/equalizer.
- SFX posture: sparse, polished; one UI click for F8, one soft reveal, one restrained final impact.
- Audio-coupled moments: boot scan, OCR result arrival, build-check rows, final logo lockup.
- Restraint rule: no loud weapon/fire sounds, no copyright-dependent game audio, no audio implying gameplay automation.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — SHD boot / hook — 3.0s
Black graphite field, cyan/orange scan line, bracket markers and “HOVER → F8 → KNOW.” Product name enters as a terminal status line. Use the real icon only as a faint silhouette until the final scene.
Sequential/interaction: yes — scan line then headline, hold the headline for readability.
Audio intent: low cinematic pulse, one clean UI tick on the F8 cue.
Audio-coupled idea: scan glow breathes with the music RMS.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — OCR card read — 5.0s
Recreate a compact Panel item-card view grounded in the app’s OCR and verdict terminology. The cursor/reticle settles on the card, OCR lines resolve, and a roll-quality bar fills.
Sequential/interaction: yes — card outline, item name, then two attribute rows; hold the full set after the stagger.
Audio intent: a precise reveal, not a game gunshot.
Audio-coupled idea: attribute rows land on every other beat.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Build-aware decision — 5.0s
Show active-build requirements and a clear KEEP / CONSIDER / REVIEW result. Include “low confidence → REVIEW” as a grounded safety cue, not an invented statistic.
Sequential/interaction: yes — requirement checks resolve one by one, then verdict badge lands on a stronger cue.
Audio intent: confident, restrained confirmation.
Audio-coupled idea: verdict badge receives the major cue near 8s.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Stash / Tasks / local-first breadth — 4.0s
Three narrow panels slide in from the real product vocabulary: Builds, Stash audit, Tasks. A small footer reads “LOCAL OCR · NO GAME MEMORY.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — three panels arrive one by one with readable labels.
Audio intent: light navigation accents only.
Audio-coupled idea: panel sequence snaps to a readable beat grid.
Transition mood: tactical wipe → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — lockup — 5.0s
Final dark terminal frame with the TD2 icon, “KEEP YOUR BUILD. REVIEW THE UNKNOWN.” and “TD2 LOOT ADVISOR · v9.1.5.35”.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: final soft impact and short music tail.
Audio-coupled idea: icon halo subtly responds to the last swell.
Transition mood: clean hold
