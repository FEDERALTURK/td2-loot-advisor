## App name

TD2 Loot Advisor Companion

## Short description

Track missions, builds, and your desktop companion status in one place.

## Full description

An independent companion app for The Division 2 players.

- Browse mission and seasonal information, and mark completed tasks on your device.
- Explore suggested builds and save your own build notes locally.
- Scan the Phone Companion QR code in TD2 Loot Advisor for read-only status from your Windows PC on the same trusted Wi-Fi network.
- Use bundled mission, news, and build content when offline.

The app has no account, ads, game-memory access, mobile OCR, or remote scan/settings controls for the desktop app. Task progress and saved builds stay on your device. The camera is used only when you open QR pairing.

Desktop pairing requires both devices to be on the same trusted private Wi-Fi network. The local connection is not encrypted. Do not expose it through router port forwarding.

This independent companion is not published, endorsed, or supported by Ubisoft or Massive Entertainment. The Division 2 and related names are trademarks of their respective owners.
